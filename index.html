ChopNow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to ChopNow. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how ChopNow collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and services.

Information We Collect

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Order information
  • Device information
  • Location data (only when permission is granted)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and deliver food orders
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Send order updates and notifications
  • Prevent fraud and maintain security

Location Services

ChopNow may request access to your location to improve delivery accuracy, show nearby restaurants, and enhance user experience. You can disable location permissions at any time through your device settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Delivery partners
  • Payment processors
  • Service providers required to operate the platform

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated information

Children's Privacy

ChopNow is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with the revised date.
