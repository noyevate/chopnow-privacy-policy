ChopNow Account and Data Deletion

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Request Account or Data Deletion

This page explains how users of the ChopNow Customer and ChopNow Restaurant applications can request deletion of their account or personal information.

ChopNow Customer App

Customers can request permanent deletion of their ChopNow account directly from within the mobile application.

Steps to Delete Your Customer Account

  1. Open the ChopNow Customer app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to your Profile.
  4. Open Settings.
  5. Select Delete Account.
  6. Follow the instructions to confirm the deletion request.

ChopNow Restaurant / Vendor App

Restaurant/vendor accounts are created or provisioned by ChopNow administrators. Restaurant users sign in using the credentials assigned to their account and cannot create a new vendor account from within the application.

Restaurant/vendor users who want their personal information deleted can submit a deletion request by email.

How to Request Vendor Data Deletion

Send an email to info@chopnowng.com using the email address associated with your ChopNow Restaurant account.

Use the email subject: ChopNow Vendor Data Deletion Request

Please provide:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your restaurant name
  • Your phone number, if associated with the account

We may contact you to verify your identity and confirm that you are authorised to make the request.

Data That May Be Deleted

Depending on the type of ChopNow account and the request made, information that may be deleted or anonymised includes:

  • Profile information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Account authentication information
  • Personal account preferences
  • Personal vendor profile information
  • Uploaded restaurant or menu content where appropriate
  • Other personal information associated with the account

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained after an account or data deletion request for legal, regulatory, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate business purposes.

This may include:

  • Completed order records
  • Payment and transaction records
  • Vendor settlement and payout records
  • Fraud-prevention and security records
  • Information required to comply with applicable laws

Information that must be retained will only be kept for as long as necessary to meet the applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, security, or operational requirement. It will be deleted or anonymised when it is no longer required.

Processing Your Request

Once a valid deletion request has been received and any necessary identity verification has been completed, ChopNow will process the request within a reasonable period.

Deleting the personal information of a restaurant user does not necessarily require deletion of the restaurant's business profile or historical business records where those records are required for legitimate operational, financial, or legal purposes.
