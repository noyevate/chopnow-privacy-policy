ChopNow Account Deletion

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

Delete Your ChopNow Account

Users can permanently delete their ChopNow account directly from within the mobile application.

Steps to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the ChopNow app
  2. Go to your Profile
  3. Open Settings
  4. Select Delete Account
  5. Confirm account deletion

What Data Is Deleted

  • Profile information
  • Saved addresses
  • Account authentication data
  • Personal account preferences

Data That May Be Retained Temporarily

Certain data may be retained temporarily for legal, financial, fraud prevention, security, and dispute resolution purposes.

Any retained information is securely deleted after the required retention period.
